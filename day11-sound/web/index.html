Every 2025 noise complaint to 311, replayed as one day. Each district is colored by its signature sound, the one it complains about most compared with the city. Hover a neighborhood to hear it (tap on phones).

How it's measured. Every 2025 noise complaint, grouped into 8 sounds. An address counts once per day per sound, so repeat callers don't swamp a neighborhood. A district's signature sound is its highest share relative to the city, among sounds 50+ different addresses there complained about.

What you hear. Each sound's loudness is the district's index for it (its share ÷ the city's share), times how busy that sound is at that hour across the city.
