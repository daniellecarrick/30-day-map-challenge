What color is a place? Hover anywhere on this satellite map of the world and the square under your cursor becomes a paint strip: its pixels sorted from light to dark and cut into seven equal parts, each chip the average color of one part. Zoom in for a single block, out for a whole region, or start with one of twelve cities. Click a chip to copy its color.

Pixels sorted from light to dark and averaged in linear RGB; city strips use a ~12 km square around each downtown. · #30DayMapChallenge
