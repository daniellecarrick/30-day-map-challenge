Whose utopia? Robert Moses drew one man's perfect city of expressways through other people's neighborhoods. Many were built. Four that never were are traced here through today's city, with every building now standing in their path.

Routes: nycroads.com histories of the Lower Manhattan and Mid-Manhattan (1955 Joint Study of Arterial Facilities), Bushwick (TBTA 1963 Future Arterial Program) and Cross-Brooklyn (1966 TBTA–Blauvelt study) expressways; NYC Building Footprints; 2020 Census blocks; built routes from OpenStreetMap · #30DayMapChallenge
